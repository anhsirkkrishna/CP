Logic: Create a functional graph. Connect the remaining nodes.
Trigger: Permutation with its index. Directed graph being formed.

In this, I did a bit complicated solution. But you could have done a bit more systemic solution.
Instead of joining some first and then some other, you can join all at once.
You can do this by treating all the non cyclic nodes as paths.
